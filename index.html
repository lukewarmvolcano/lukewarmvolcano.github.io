Who's Behind The Lens

Fifteen Years
Behind The Lens.

Lukewarm Volcano is David Kelly's freelance videography studio, based in Kilrush, County Clare, working as a videographer, camera operator and drone operator across Munster and Ireland. Over fifteen years shooting and editing, from brand films to music videos to community documentaries, he's built a style around natural light, honest performances, and giving every project the same close attention — whether you're launching a brand, sharing a story, or documenting a moment worth remembering.
