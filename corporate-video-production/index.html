Corporate Video Questions

How long does a corporate video project take?

Most projects go from first call to delivered film in one to three weeks, depending on shoot days and revisions needed.

Do you film on location or in a studio?

On location at your business is standard — this keeps the video authentic and avoids the cost of studio hire.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes a revision pass as standard; additional rounds are quoted up front so there are no surprises.
