A Promo That Keeps Getting Watched

West Coast Aqua Park needed a video that sold the fun of the place in the time someone spends scrolling past it. It’s since passed 16,000 views on YouTube.

Client
West Coast Aqua Park
Sector
Tourism & leisure
Services
Drone & promo video
Location
Killaloe, Co. Clare

The brief

West Coast Aqua Park is Killaloe’s floating water park — inflatable obstacles out on the water, aimed at families and groups looking for something active. A video like that has one job: make someone scrolling on their phone want to book a slot that afternoon.

The shoot

Drone-led, because the park only really makes sense from above — the layout, the scale of it, people falling off things into the water. Fast, high-energy, cut for someone with about ten seconds before they scroll past.

Since it went out

It’s the best-performing video on the channel so far, past 16,000 views on YouTube. Still doing work well beyond the summer it was shot for.
