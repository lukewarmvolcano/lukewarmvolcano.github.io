Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you contact Lukewarm Volcano or use this website. It is written in plain English rather than legal boilerplate, because you should be able to actually read it.

Last updated 14 August 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

Lukewarm Volcano is the trading name used by David Kelly, based in Kilrush, County Clare, Ireland. David is the data controller for any personal information you share. You can reach him at david@lukewarmvolcano.com or on 083-4704-688.

“Lukewarm Volcano” is a business name registered with the Companies Registration Office, Ireland, under registration number 661934.

What this website collects

This website has no account system, no newsletter sign-up and no analytics software. It does not build a profile of you, and it sets no cookies of its own.

There is one form, on the quote page. If you fill it in, it collects your name, business name, email address, phone number if you give it, your location, and whatever you tell us about the project, along with rough budget and timeline if you select them. Everything on that form is optional except your name, email and a description of the project — enough to write back to you with a sensible answer.

You can skip the form entirely and email or ring instead. Nothing is lost by doing so.

Why we hold it, and for how long

Enquiry details are used for one purpose: to reply to you, quote for the work, and deliver the project if you go ahead. The lawful basis is legitimate interest for an initial reply, and performance of a contract once you book.

Emails and project files are kept for as long as the working relationship is active, and afterwards for as long as Irish tax law requires where the records relate to invoicing. Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted once they are clearly no longer live.

Form submissions arrive as an ordinary email and are treated exactly like an email you sent yourself — same purpose, same retention, same deletion.

Your details are never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Photography and video of people

Filming produces footage that may include identifiable people. Where a shoot takes place on private premises or involves staff, customers or attendees, permission is agreed with the client before filming, and anyone who asks not to appear is left out of the final edit. If you appear in footage and want it removed, contact David directly.

Third-party services this site uses

Three external services are involved in running this website, and it is worth being straight about what each one means:

YouTube. Videos on this site are hosted on YouTube and embedded through youtube-nocookie.com, which suppresses advertising cookies. Nothing loads from YouTube until you click play — until then, no request is made and no data leaves your browser. Once you press play, Google receives your IP address and player interaction data under its own privacy policy.

Google Fonts. Typefaces are served from Google's font servers, which means your IP address is visible to Google when the page loads. No cookies are set. This is a common setup, but it is a genuine data transfer and you are entitled to know about it.

Web3Forms. The quote form is handled by Web3Forms, which takes the submission and forwards it to David’s email inbox. It exists so that a website with no server of its own can still send you a reply. Their servers are in the United States, so submitting the form transfers your details outside the EEA. Web3Forms is not used for anything else, receives nothing unless you press send, and there is no tracking attached to it. If you would rather your details did not pass through a US service, email david@lukewarmvolcano.com directly instead.

Cookies

Lukewarm Volcano sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts, which is why you are not being asked to dismiss a consent banner. If analytics are ever added, this policy will be updated and consent will be requested before anything is loaded.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask for a copy of the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, object to how it is used, or ask for it to be sent elsewhere. Email david@lukewarmvolcano.com and you will get a response within one month.

If you are unhappy with how a request is handled, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
