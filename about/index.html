I'm David.

Director & Editor, Lukewarm Volcano

Filmmaking is the thing I'd do even if nobody paid me for it. There's nothing else that would get me out of bed for free, so I count myself lucky that it's the job.

I run Lukewarm Volcano on my own from Kilrush, County Clare — every shoot, every edit, every client conversation, me start to finish. I'm drawn to community-focused projects, documentaries and Irish history most of all: the stories that matter to the people in them, not just to an audience scrolling past.

What I Believe In
Being Creative

Every project deserves its own idea, not a template — I'd rather find the angle that makes it interesting than repeat what worked last time.

Helping People

A good video should do something real for the person or business it's made for, not just look good.

Doing Good Business

Straight quotes, clear communication, and work delivered when I say it will be — no surprises.

Enjoying What I Do

I chose this because I love it. That doesn't leave the room on a shoot day, and it shows in the work.
